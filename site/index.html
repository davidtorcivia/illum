illum

Ornament for web pages, written by a simulated pen

  1. Blackletter, from German books of the fifteenth century
  2. Tarot, from the Tarot de Marseille of Nicolas Conver, 1760
  3. Occult, from French copies of the Key of Solomon
  4. Astrology, from William Lilly’s Christian Astrology, 1647
  5. Draft, from the Apollo drawings of the MIT Instrumentation Lab

Once a scribe had finished a book, a rubricator went over it with red ink and a finer pen. illum does the same for a web page. It finds the openings, paragraphs, breaks and quotations in the page’s markup, and a simulated pen writes a mark for each in ink.

The text is shown first and left as it is. The ornament is drawn behind it a moment later, and the same text always gets the same ornament.

You must either make a tool of the creature, or a man of him. You cannot make both.
