Gold

A study of large gold, after research/07-gold.md and three rejected attempts.

Live

Gold lit as gold is: broad sweeps of reflection over an undulating ground, dark olive where the leaf turns away from the light at its edges, and the real detail (cracks, specks, burnishing strokes) of a photographed ground. Move the pointer, or a finger, across it and the light travels over the leaf; let go and it rests.

Fallback

Without WebGL2: the light from broad sweeps of reflection over an undulating ground, dark olive where the leaf turns away from the light at its edges; the hand from the real detail (cracks, specks, burnishing strokes) of a photographed ground, Met 477838. At size, then three times, the detail at three scales.

Rejected

The earlier attempt, kept for comparison: a raised cushion of gesso as a height field, lit. It read as 1999 WordArt.
