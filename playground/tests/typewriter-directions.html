The typewriter · variegated typewriting

Typewriter art and the office typist’s craft as a rubrication. One machine types the page: its every key has its own faults, the same on every strike; the typist’s force drifts slowly and is spread over each glyph’s area; the ribbon is drawn down by a run of strikes and wicks back in the pauses; the weave of the cloth and the fibre of the sheet take the ink unevenly; overstrikes add. Each colour is poured through the ink engine and laid on the stock: ribbon inks on bond in light, a cover-up film on black card in dark. Letters: Courier Prime (Alan Dague-Greene, OFL); text in EB Garamond. The strike, on a bench.
