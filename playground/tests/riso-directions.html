Duplicator · the overprint

Stencil-duplicator printing: two drums, one pass. Federal Blue carries the letter and the strips, Fluorescent Pink its cut masses over them, and where they cross the inks make violet. Every shape is cut, as a separation is: knife runs and scissor arcs meeting at corners; no compass. The ink is poured through the ink engine: it creeps a little into the fibre, carries the drum’s streaks along the feed and a slow mottle, and its film’s density varies continuously at the paper fibre’s scale (no dots, no pinholes). The seed sets the page’s hand, which every mark follows. Letter: Gloock (Duarte Pinto, OFL); text in EB Garamond. Ink colours after stencil.wiki (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). The press, on a bench.
