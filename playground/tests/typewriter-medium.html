The strike, on a bench

Each patch is typed by kit/styles/typewriter-machine.js exactly as the directions are: one machine per page (the seed), every key with its own faults; each strike’s ink set by the typist’s force over the glyph’s area and by what the ribbon has left; the weave of the cloth under it; overstrikes added in the deposit; each colour’s deposit poured into the ink engine (kit/medium.js) and its film laid on the stock (filtering the paper in light; an opaque cover-up film on black card in dark). Letters: Courier Prime (Alan Dague-Greene, OFL). The directions.
