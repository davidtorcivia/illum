# sumi-refs: credits

Crops of real ink-brush works, shown beside our brush on sumi-medium.html and measured for research/28-sumi.md (Stage 1).
Used to measure and to look at, never shipped as texture. Scale is the crop's pixels per millimetre on the work, and how we
know it.

| file | work | source | rights | scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bankei-enso.jpg | Bankei Yōtaku (1622–1693), ensō with inscription (the circle only, cropped clear of the inscription; its seal, at the top right, blanked with the paper's median colour) | Wikimedia Commons, File:Bankeitumblr lm43mrAJZc1qzgealo1 1280Sakyamuni&Maitreya.jpg (1280 × 660) | public domain by age (the work); the file is a re-upload from a tumblr post, not a museum scan, so its colour and sharpness are unknown | unknown: the work's size is not given; measured in fractions of the circle |

| zhengxie-leaves.jpg | Zheng Xie (1693–1766), *Orchids and bamboo*, dated 1742, handscroll, ink on paper, 34.9 cm high (leaves at the right, cropped clear of the inscription) | The Metropolitan Museum of Art, 1981.285.7 (object 49244), Open Access image DP204895 | CC0 | ~5.8 px/mm, measured: the scroll's 34.9 cm height spans ~2028 px |

The seals on these scrolls were measured (colour) but not cropped: a seal carries its owner's characters.

Not reproduced, studied for principle only (in copyright): Morita Shiryū, Inoue Yūichi, Shinoda Tōkō, Franz Kline,
Kazuaki Tanahashi. Not used: the Commons ensō files by Kanjuro Shibata XX (CC BY-SA, a living tradition's working
calligrapher) and Thich Nhat Hanh (CC BY 4.0).

Note: the dry leaf at the left of zhengxie-leaves.jpg shows real dry brush as broken flecks. That is true to the paper,
and it is exactly the texture the house has rejected ("ugly dots"); our dry brush is modelled on the continuous streaks of
Bankei's lower right instead.
