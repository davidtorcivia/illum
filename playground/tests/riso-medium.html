The press, on a bench

Each patch is printed by kit/styles/riso-press.js exactly as the directions are: stencils per drum, drum 2 through the sheet’s one miss, each drum’s stencil poured into the ink engine (kit/medium.js: the soy ink creeps a fraction of a pixel into the fibre at its edge, levels and dries; a thin film catches only the paper tooth’s peaks; the drum lays its own streaks along the feed and a slow mottle), its film read back and composited by the film law (translucent inks filter, opaque inks hide; the second drum takes 80 % on the first’s wet film). ?ink=flat shows the stage-1 compositor.
