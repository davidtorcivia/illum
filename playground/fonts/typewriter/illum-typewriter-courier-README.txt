illum-typewriter-courier-regular.woff2 and illum-typewriter-courier-bold.woff2 are subsets of Courier Prime (Alan
Dague-Greene, Quote-Unquote Apps; Copyright 2015 The Courier Prime Project Authors, https://github.com/quoteunquoteapps/CourierPrime)
(printable ASCII, the pilcrow, section sign, middle dot, multiplication sign, daggers, dashes, quotes and ellipsis only),
made for illum with fontTools (pyftsubset) and renamed Illum Typewriter Courier. Courier Prime has no Reserved Font Name;
the subset is renamed only so that it is never mistaken for the original. The original font, its copyright and its
licence: CourierPrime-OFL.txt (beside this file). Cutive Mono (CutiveMono-*) beside them serves only the stage-1 study
pages (?face=cutive) and is not shipped.
