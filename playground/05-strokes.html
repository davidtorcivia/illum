Real strokes

Brush and pen strokes cut from open-access images of real leaves and scrolls: the painted capitals and penwork of choir books, and the ink of Chinese and Japanese calligraphy and bamboo painting. Each keeps its own paint, its pooling and its dry streaks; the paper is taken away. Hover a stroke for its centerline, which starts at the loaded end.

Every image here is from The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access or the Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access, both public domain (CC0); the object, title, date and licence of each stroke are in materials/strokes/manifest.json. Cut by tools/strokes/: the ground estimated per region, the paint unmixed from it, each stroke's skeleton traced from tip to tip, and then chosen by hand.
