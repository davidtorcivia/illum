Lombardic

A drawn alphabet: each capital made from the strokes a scribe makes, in order, each with its swell; then painted as the initials were painted, in a dozen decisions. Blue and red alternate, as they do down a page.

Four times, beside the real thing

The same letters at four times, each beside a painted capital of the same letter from a real leaf.

In use

But our higher instincts are not deceived. We take no pleasure in the building provided for us, resembling that which we take in a new book or a new picture. We may be proud of its size, complacent in its correctness, and happy in its convenience. We may take the same pleasure in its symmetry and workmanship as in a well-ordered room, or a skilful piece of manufacture. And this we suppose to be all the pleasure that architecture was ever intended to give us.
