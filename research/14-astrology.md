# 14 · Astrology: what makes a page read as an astrologer's book

The fourth style done deep, after Blackletter (11), Tarot (12) and Occult (13). A breadth-first Astrology was rejected
(cases.html?style=astrology; kit/wayfinding.js's Astrology entry): navy and gold, a round wheel of hairline spokes, rows of
tiny coloured lozenges for fillers, a navy Garamond B. None of it was read from a source: no period sign forms, no square
chart, no ruled tables, a palette (navy and gold) that the printed and manuscript sources do not have, and every line a
hairline. This is what was studied before kit/styles/astrology.js was written.

## Measurements

- **The square figure** (Lilly img 66 and 185, Purslow img 14): the middle square's side is **half the outer square's**,
  its corners on the diamond through the middles of the outer sides; the outer corners ruled in to the inner corners. The
  twelve lines out of the inner corners are the cusps (house 1 on the left, the eastern angle, counted counter-clockwise).
  Ruling: Purslow's pen **~0.4 % of the side** (7–9 px on ~1900 px), Lilly's printed rule ~0.5 %; one weight throughout.
- **The cusps' writing**: the sign and its degree and minute written **along the line**, the sign first, reading outward
  or left to right, a hair off the line (Purslow) or on it (Lilly), separated by `·`, `:` or `=`; the sign ~5–6 % of the
  side, the figures a little smaller. Planets with their degrees written inside the houses. Lilly is all black; Purslow one
  brown ink, **[130 101 75]** on paper [234 219 185].
- **The ephemeris** (Lilly img 27): a thick rule and a thin under the month's title, a thin double rule under the column
  heads; each column headed by a planet sign over the zodiac sign it is in, with "M D" over; rules between columns, a rule
  under every pair of days.
- **The degree ring** (Apian DT5528): two thin rules ~**1:1.6** (band : cell length), the cells alternately inked and
  left, a numeral every 5 cells; the Clavis volvelle's ring is the same, irregular in length. Rule ~0.3 % of the diameter.
- **The volvelle of aspects** (Clavis): ring of the signs ~20 % of the radius, the degree ring ~7 %, then a double circle;
  the signs upright to the centre (those at the foot upside down to the reader); the aspect signs ~10 % of the radius,
  threaded on their lines about two-thirds of the way out.
- **The signs as cut** (Clavis): ♈ two curls on a stem; ♉ a ring with horns; ♊ a heavy II; ♋ two curls; ♌ an S-loop; ♍ an
  *m* with a looped first stroke (written "au"); ♎ a hump on a bar over a second bar; ♏ the *m* with a hooked "z" before
  it; ♐ a hooked 3 with a dart across; ♑ a V run into a loop (Lilly's "Vƨ"); ♒ two zigzags; ♓ two arcs back to back on a
  bar. Strokes ~12 % of the sign's height: heavy, as woodcut type is.
- **Colour**: two inks. Black or brown-black for the ruling, the figures and the text; red (red lead going to vermilion,
  [198 87 52] … [179 101 71]) for **the names and the signs** in Knodius's wheel and the calendars, for the headings and
  numerals of the rubricated tables, for the eclipse. No gold except Apian's hand-colouring; no blue, no navy.

## What makes it read as an astrologer's page (the checklist the style is judged by)

1. **The figure of heaven**: the square chart's exact geometry, the middle square for the capital, the cusps written
   along their lines with their degrees — or the volvelle's compass circles, the ring of signs, the checkered degree ring.
2. **The signs as the period cut them**, heavy, not the modern Unicode forms (♍ as "au", ♑ as "Vƨ", ♐ as the hooked dart).
3. **Ruled tables**: the ephemeris's thick-and-thin head, the columns, a sign at the head of each.
4. **Two inks**: brown-black for construction and figures, red for signs, names and capitals.
5. **The aspects threaded on lines**, drawn from one place.

## What was made from it (kit/styles/astrology.js, playground/tests/astrology.html)

Every rule and circle is written by the ink engine's pen (kit/writer.js) as one stroke (a compass circle from its top
round and over its start; a rule a little past its ends); the pen-written signs are continuous strokes with merging joins
(sketch(), after occult.js). The zodiac (traced) and the lettering (IM FELL Double Pica, the Oxford types of the 1670s;
figures in EB Garamond, whose 4 reads as 4 where the Fell's reads as +) are poured as coverage by kit/inkletter.js; on a
still page the small lettering of one ink in one case is poured as one coverage (clusters ≤ 240 px), written one by one
under ?draw. Nothing under 1.3 px. Tools: **tools/upright.py** (turn a scan about a ring's centre so a sign printed round
it stands upright, and crop it) and **tools/traceshape.py** (a woodcut's ink, too heavy for tracesign.py's local-median
test, as polygons).

- Drop initial (the user's choice, C: the square figure, 6 lines deep by the style's own `initial.lines`; the host's
  data-lines rules): the IM FELL capital in red in the middle square, under it a line in the italic as Lilly writes the
  day there ("Anno Dom: 1853", `title`); each of the twelve cusps' signs in red along its line with its degree in black;
  planets with their degrees written small in the houses where they fit. Every label is kept wholly inside its house,
  its ink (inkOf: pen points with their nib, polygon vertices, the text's ink box from the face's metrics) at least the
  rule's width + 1.5 px (and its bow) from every rule's centre: a cusp's label is tried along its line; if it will not go
  it is written smaller, then with its degree alone, then without figures, then left out; a planet is tried over a grid
  in the house, upright or along a slanting edge, clear of what is written; a house with no room stays empty, as Lilly's
  do. Figures never under 7 px; under ~9 px a planet would be a blot, so on a phone the figure keeps its cusps only.
  tests/astrology.check.mjs (with tests/astrology-check.html) measures every label against the drawn rules and its house
  over 12 seeds × data-lines 4–8 × the 1440 and 390 type sizes: 0 crossings. ?set=volvelle: the round
  form, a ring of the signs round a checkered degree ring.
- The signs: the zodiac traced from the Clavis woodcut (♋ ♎ ♑ pen-written in its proportions); the planets ☉ ☽ ☿ ♀ ♂ ♃ ♄ ☊
  pen-written in the forms of Lilly's ephemeris head (traced from its 45 px type they were blots above 20 px); the five
  aspects pen-written. tests/astrology-signs.html shows all at 10, 15, 18 and 36 px.
- Line initial: the IM FELL capital in red. Paraph: the chapter's sign (Aries the first; before it, Pisces).
- Fillers: **aspects** (☌ ⚹ □ △ ☍ threaded on a rule, the rule running on through each as on the volvelle), **signs**
  (the twelve in a ruled band, a cell each), **degrees** (Apian's checkered scale, a tick every fifth cell, its band ≥ 5 px
  so the paper cells hold at dpr 1); a heading's: a rule ending in the chapter's sign.
- Breaks: chapter, a small volvelle on a ruled band lettered CAP. I. and ARIES; book, the Clavis volvelle as two papers:
  the fixed disc (ring of signs, degree ring) and the turning plate with the aspects threaded on lines from the ascendant,
  the square and trine inscribed, and its index, a narrow pointer cut with the plate and run out over the degree ring to
  the middle of the ascendant's sign. The pointer is paper laid over the rings: the circles and cells under it are hidden
  (and a hair either side), its edge one pen stroke out to the point and back; a rivet at the centre, the lines stopping
  at it.
- Frames: code, Lilly's ephemeris head (thick and thin rules; the day column; eight planets in red each headed M and D, or
  A, over their signs; two days' rows of degrees and minutes in the planets' own motions, a rule under them); figure, Apian's
  octagon (the user's choice), its band checkered as his degree rings, broken at head and foot by a small ring with the
  chapter's sign and its opposite (?figure=houses: the twelve houses unrolled into a band round the figure); pull quote, two arcs of a checkered ring broken by signs;
  quotation, the table's thick-and-thin ruling; aside, a NOTA head band between aspect signs; contents, the ephemeris's
  day column.
- Palette: light k [56 42 32], r [178 62 36]; dark k [226 214 194], r [236 120 86].
- ?draw: per case the black (ruling, then the planets' pens), then the red, then the lettering, as each case's line comes
  15 % into view; never scrolls (the reader is tests/occult.html's of before rubricate(): the style is not in the registry).

What falls short (at the 4× gate): the figure is still sparer than Lilly's (five planets, not seven, and no aspects or
part of fortune in the houses); the cusp writing beside the line, not on it as Lilly prints it; the planets are a pen's
redrawing of Lilly's type, not a trace; the octagon frame, though both its parts are Apian's, is not a frame he drew round
a rectangle; the volvelle's plate is drawn with a compass where the paper plate was cut by hand.
